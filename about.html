About Us

Welcome to Image Compressor, an online tool designed to make image optimization simple and convenient.

What We Do

Our tool helps users compress and resize images using adjustable dimensions, quality settings and file-size targets. It supports JPG, PNG and WebP image formats.

Our Goal

Our goal is to provide a straightforward, accessible image optimization experience for everyday users, website owners, students and content creators.

Privacy-Focused Processing

Image processing is performed directly in your browser. Images selected for compression are not uploaded to our server by the compressor tool.
